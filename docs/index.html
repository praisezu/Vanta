Get it running

Windows

Download the installer, pick your Discord, press Install. Takes about half a minute, and Discord reopens with Vanta in its settings.

Download for Windows

macOS and Linux

Paste this into Terminal. It checks the download against the release's checksum before running anything.

curl -fsSL https://tryvanta.dev/install.sh | sh

Changed your mind? The same installer takes Vanta out and puts Discord back the way it was. Your settings stay in the Vanta folder in case you come back.
